A traditional sweet drink called _amazake_ is becoming increasingly popular across Japan for its health benefits. Once seen mainly as a sweet, warm drink for the winter, _amazake_ is now being promoted as a drink that can be enjoyed hot or cold all year round. And it may be about to go global!

_Amazake_ means "sweet sake" in Japanese — but it actually contains little or no alcohol, depending on how it's made.

There are two types of _amazake_. One type, _sake kasu amazake_, is made with sake lees — called _kasu_ in Japanese — which is the solid mass left over from the rice and other ingredients used to make sake. This type of _amazake_ is made by mixing the lees with hot water and sugar, and it contains trace amounts of alcohol.

The other type, _koji amazake_, is a fermented mix of rice, water and malted rice, which is called _koji_ in Japanese. This drink is naturally sweet, needing no extra sugar, and contains no alcohol at all.

_Koji amazake_  is seen as being particularly healthy because it contains probiotics — bacteria and yeasts that are good for gut health. It also has many types of vitamin B — including vitamin B2, which is good for your skin, hair and nails — as well as amino acids that are also good for the skin, and lots of glucose, which gives you extra energy.

_Koji amazake_ is increasingly being promoted as a cold drink that can help keep people hydrated in summer. According to _The Yomiuri Shimbun_, an _amazake_ vending machine was even installed in June in a childcare center in the famously hot city of Kumagaya in Saitama prefecture.

And Ferment Base, a new company based in the city of Utsunomiya in Tochigi prefecture, has been promoting the health benefits of _amazake_ abroad. The company has developed a smoother, lighter type of _amazake_, which it believes will appeal more to foreign tastes. The company made its _amazake_ available at some California supermarkets in January.
