Most of us spend a large part of our lives at work, so it's important to feel happy with what we do!

But according to analysis from London's Resolution Foundation, many people in the UK are not satisfied with their jobs.

In fact, job satisfaction is at an all-time low.

The analysis is based on the 2025 British Social Attitudes survey, which had over {{prn:4,600|four thousand and six hundred}} participants. The survey results were published on November 3 this year.

The data showed that just 74% of British workers are satisfied with their jobs. This is the lowest percentage since the question was first asked in 1989.

When asked if they have an interesting job, just 63% said yes — a decrease of 11 percentage points from 10 years ago.

There are several reasons why people may be feeling less satisfied with their jobs.

Many workers feel their salaries aren't good, especially with rising costs in the UK and other parts of the world.

Others are unhappy with their working hours — 61% say they want more flexibility, up from 46% 10 years ago.

People are also less willing to do extra work for their employers.

Ten years ago, 69% of workers said they were happy to work harder to help their company. Now it's just 55%.

According to the Resolution Foundation, this decrease in job satisfaction is bad news for both employees and employers.

And John Curtice, who worked on the British Social Attitudes survey, said employers may need to offer better pay and more flexible working hours to keep employees happy and motivated.
