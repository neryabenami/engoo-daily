After a 13-year wait, _Grand Theft Auto VI_ has been released worldwide, marking one of the most anticipated entertainment launches in years.

Players in New Zealand were among the first to explore the game, which was released gradually across the world as midnight arrived on November 19.

"The gameplay dynamics are just amazing and the graphics are great and you can tell that the storytelling is really deep," said Adam Simmons from Wellington after about half an hour of play.

The _Grand Theft Auto_ franchise has sold more than 470 million copies worldwide. _Grand Theft Auto {{prn:V|five}}_, released in 2013, accounts for more than 230 million of those sales and generated $1 billion in retail sales within just three days of its release.

Developed by Rockstar Games, _GTA {{prn:VI|six}}_ returns to Vice City, a fictional version of Miami, and features the company's largest and most detailed open world yet.

The story follows two protagonists, Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, a couple whose attempts to improve their lives pull them deeper into the criminal underworld.

The focus on their relationship marks a major departure from _GTA {{prn:V|five}}_ which followed three playable protagonists.

While developing the game, Rockstar's teams explored Miami and surrounding areas, researching the region's history and architecture.

Miami has also embraced its connection to Vice City. Miami-Dade County entered into a $3.9 million promotional deal with Rockstar, while the Miami Heat basketball team planned to wear special black-and-pink Vice City uniforms.

However, some local officials have questioned whether public resources should be used to promote a franchise known for car theft and other criminal activities.

Few entertainment releases have arrived with this much anticipation. The first _GTA VI_ trailer drew more than 93 million YouTube views in its first 24 hours.

Even before its global release, the game received eight nominations for the Game Awards, including game of the year.

Shares of Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar's parent company, rose more than 8% on the day before the launch.
