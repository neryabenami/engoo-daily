Link must protect the kingdom of Hyrule from the evil Ganondorf — but luckily, he has millions of players around the world ready to help him!

The remake of Nintendo's _The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time_ was released on November 5, and it sold more than 5.2 million copies around the world in its first four days. This includes {{prn:810,000|eight hundred and ten thousand}} copies sold in Japan.

This makes it the fastest-selling game released by Nintendo for its Switch 2 console so far. The sales figures include both physical and digital copies.

The original game was released in 1998 for the Nintendo 64. In the game, players have time-travel powers that allow them to play across two time periods as young Link and adult Link.

When the game was first released, it was considered a huge breakthrough in video games, introducing many players to a huge 3D world they could explore.

It went on to become one of Nintendo's highest-rated games.

_The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time_ was re-released for the Nintendo 3DS in 2011, with better graphics and controls, but it largely kept the original game's look and feel.

The new version has been recreated for the Switch 2, with updated graphics, new music and improved camera and movement controls. It also includes new voiced cutscenes and more dialogue.

As in the original, music plays an important role in the game. Link carries an ocarina — a small wind instrument — which he uses to play special melodies that help him solve puzzles and continue his adventure.

In the remake, players can even use the Switch 2's microphone to hum or play these tunes themselves!

Nintendo says the game is a "great jumping-off point to learn what the series is all about," especially for people who have never played any Zelda games before.

The game is available exclusively for the Nintendo Switch 2, and costs {{prn:$59.99|fifty nine dollars and ninety nine cents}}.
