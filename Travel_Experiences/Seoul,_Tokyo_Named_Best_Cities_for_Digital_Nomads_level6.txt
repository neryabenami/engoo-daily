Modern technology has made it possible for some people to do their jobs from almost anywhere in the world.

And a new ranking has revealed the best cities for people who like to work remotely while traveling — also known as "digital nomads."

VPN review platform {{prn:vpn.jpn.com|VPN dot JPN dot com}} ranked {{prn:104|one hundred and four}} cities around the world based on things that are important for digital nomads.

These include things like cost, how easy it is to work from there, internet speeds and things to do in the city.

Seoul was named the best city in the world for digital nomads. The South Korean capital has plenty of places for people to work from, and it's a great city for people who like to go out and visit cultural places.

Seoul also has the fastest internet download speed of all the cities in the top 10.

In second place was Tokyo — one of three Japanese cities in the top 10. Tokyo also has plenty of places to work from, but it's a little less affordable than Seoul.

However, Tokyo has the largest number of going-out options of all the cities in the top 10, making it a great place for digital nomads who like to do things after they finish work.

Japan was the only country to have three cities in the top 10, with Osaka ranking fifth and Nagoya ninth.

Asia was very strong, with Vietnam's Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City — and Taiwan's Taipei — also in the top 10.

Spain was the only European country with cities in the top 10. Two of its cities got high scores largely thanks to Spain having the fastest internet upload speed of the top 15 cities.

Barcelona, which has plenty of going-out options, ranked sixth, while the capital, Madrid, followed in seventh place.
