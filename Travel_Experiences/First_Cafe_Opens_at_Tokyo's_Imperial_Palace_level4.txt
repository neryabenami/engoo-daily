Tokyo's Imperial Palace is one of the city's most famous places. People like to go there to see the gardens and learn about Japan's history.

And now they can have a coffee there, too!

On November 3, a cafe opened at the Imperial Palace. It's the first time a cafe has opened inside the palace grounds.

The cafe is part of Sarutahiko Coffee, a well-known coffee company in Tokyo. It's inside the Ote Rest House, a new building in the palace's East Gardens.

Visitors can have their coffee inside the cafe or on the terrace on the second floor, which has nice views of the gardens.

The company made a new type of coffee just for this cafe, and visitors can also buy cakes and other sweet things.

Inside the new building, visitors can learn about the history of the Imperial Palace and the Imperial Family.

There's also a shop where visitors can buy souvenirs, or get some more coffee to make at home!

The cafe is near the Museum of the Imperial Collections. This museum has been closed for seven years, but it opened again on November 3.

More than 2 million people visited the East Gardens in 2025. You don't need to pay to go there — but you will have to pay for your coffee!
