When you're trying to feel calmer and more focused, could the answer be right under your feet?

Research from Kyushu University in Japan has found that  _igusa_, the grass used to make the _tatami_ mats found in traditional Japanese floors, could have a positive effect on stress and concentration.

The research, published in _Scientific Reports_, involved 17 participants who were asked to do the same task with two different types of mats.

The task tested participants' ability to distinguish between various images. In this case, they looked at different sets of gray lines while their brain activity was monitored.

They did the task in a soundproof room. First, some people did it with a real _tatami_ mat, made with _igusa_, on the floor, while others had a fake _tatami_ mat made of plastic. Then, two weeks later, they did the same thing again in the same room, but with the opposite mat.

Both times, the participants answered questions on how they felt before and after doing the task, as well as how they felt about how the room smelled.

Smell was something the researchers were careful about. They also asked participants not to wear perfumes or other scented products, and had them change into scent-free laboratory clothes.

It was found that participants performed better on the image task when they had _igusa_ mats, answering more quickly without any increase in errors. The brain activity data showed that people were more focused on the task when they had _igusa_ mats too.

The researchers suggested that this could be because of the calming scent of the _igusa_.

The participants generally rated the room as smelling better with an _igusa_ mat as well — and people's mood reports also showed that they tended to feel less stress and anxiety when there was an _igusa_ mat in the room.

So if you're feeling stressed and can't focus, maybe the first thing you need to change is your floor!
