Although tai chi as an exercise is around 400 years old, the philosophical principles behind this popular and gentle practice from China are thought to go back thousands of years.

Tai chi, along with other similar traditional Chinese exercises, has long been believed to offer a number of health benefits. A major review has now confirmed that these exercises, and tai chi especially, really can help people experiencing {{prn:musculoskeletal|musculo skeletal}} disorders.

{{prn:Musculoskeletal|musculo skeletal}}  disorders include a range of conditions that can affect your muscles, bones and joints. More than 1.7 billion people around the world have some kind of  {{prn:musculoskeletal|musculo skeletal}} disorder, and they are the leading contributor to disability.

The review, published in _British Journal of Sports Medicine_, used data from over  {{prn:1,700|one thousand and seven hundred}} clinical trials looking at the health benefits of traditional Chinese exercises.

The researchers found "high certainty evidence" that exercises like tai chi were effective in helping people with a range of different  {{prn:musculoskeletal|musculo skeletal}} disorders.

The health benefits included improving movement for people with osteoarthritis of the knee — a painful condition in which joint cartilage gradually breaks down. Traditional Chinese exercises were also associated with better mental health outcomes for people with this condition, as well as for those with neck pain.

These exercises were also linked with better sleep for people with fibromyalgia — a long-term condition, thought to be caused by changes in the nervous system, that causes pain across the body.

Tai chi stood out in particular for how it significantly eased the chronic pain and stiffness associated with several disorders.

It was also linked with increased bone mineral density among people with certain conditions in which the bones get weaker due to density loss.

The results of the review support traditional Chinese exercises being integrated into approaches to treat and manage  {{prn:musculoskeletal|musculo skeletal}} disorders.

The researchers said that traditional Chinese exercises are safe, accessible and cost-effective, and can complement conventional medical treatments for  {{prn:musculoskeletal|musculo skeletal}} disorders.

They also said the exercises can empower patients and reduce how much they need to rely on drugs to manage their disorder.
