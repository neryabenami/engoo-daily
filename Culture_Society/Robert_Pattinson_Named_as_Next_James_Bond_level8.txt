Robert Pattinson.

After years of speculation about who would replace Daniel Craig as the iconic secret agent, British actor Robert Pattinson has been named the next James Bond.

Amazon MGM Studios announced the decision on November 16, making Pattinson the seventh actor to play {{prn:007|double-oh seven}} in the series' 64-year history.

"James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with {{prn:007|double-oh seven}} is the honour of a lifetime," Pattinson said in a statement.

Pattinson, 40, will star in the 26th Bond film, directed by Denis Villeneuve. Filming is expected to begin in 2027, although no official start date has been announced.

Villeneuve said it was a tough choice, but the right one.

"He has the timeless soul of {{prn:007|double-oh seven}} and brings a spirit that is completely new," the director said of Pattinson.

Pattinson first became internationally famous through the _Twilight_ films and has since built a varied career, appearing in major franchises such as _The Batman_, _Harry Potter_ and _Dune_, as well as smaller independent films including _The Lighthouse_ and _Mickey 17_. He is also currently filming _The Batman Part II_.

Pattinson takes over from Daniel Craig, who played Bond in five films between 2006 and 2021. Craig's final appearance was in _No Time to Die_, which ended with the character's death.

The film also comes at a pivotal moment for the franchise.

In 2022, Amazon bought Bond's longtime studio MGM for {{prn:$8.5 billion|eight point five billion dollars}}. Three years later, Bond producers Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson gave up creative control of the franchise.

The next installment will be the first Bond film made under Amazon's leadership.

The James Bond films, based on novels by British author Ian Fleming, have grossed more than {{prn:$7 billion|seven billion dollars}} worldwide in ticket sales, making the film series one of Hollywood's most lucrative.
