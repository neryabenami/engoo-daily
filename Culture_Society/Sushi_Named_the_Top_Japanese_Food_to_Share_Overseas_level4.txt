What do you think of when you hear "Japanese food"?

This is what 600 people in Japan were asked in a survey by Suzumo Machinery.

And eight in 10 people said they think of sushi, making it the top answer!

Tempura came second, named by 57% of people. Miso soup was third, named by 44%. Next were udon, soba and onigiri.

The survey also asked people in Japan what Japanese food they want people in other countries to taste.

Sushi was again the top answer, named by 68% of people. Tempura came second, named by 42%.

_Shabu-shabu_ and _sukiyaki_ were next, followed by ramen and {{prn:_onigiri_|onigheeri}}.

But while miso soup was third on the list of food people in Japan think of, it was only eighth on the list of food they want people in other countries to taste.

Sushi is already very popular outside Japan, and there are many sushi restaurants in other countries. So what's the most important thing when making sushi for people outside Japan?

Seventy percent of people said the taste and quality are the most important things — and 35% said taste and quality are important even if they make the sushi more expensive.

Just 16% said it's more important that the sushi is cheap.

The survey shows that Japanese people are happy to share their food with the world — but it has to taste right!
