Japanese monster movie _Godzilla Minus Zero_ topped the North American box office over the weekend, earning {{prn:$31.6 million|thirty one point six million dollars}} and setting a new opening record for a live-action foreign-language film.

Takashi Yamazaki's sequel to 2023's _Godzilla Minus One_ is set in Japan after World War II. It continues one of cinema's longest-running franchises, with Godzilla recently celebrating his 72nd birthday.

The film broke the previous opening record for a live-action foreign-language movie, held by 2004's _Hero_, which earned $17.9 million, and added to the trend of international, non-English productions drawing American audiences to cinemas.

_Godzilla Minus Zero_ also earned about $17 million in Japan, where it opened at the same time as in North America.

"This historic opening shows how deeply audiences around the world connect with Godzilla and with Takashi Yamazaki's vision," said Koji Ueda, president of Toho Global, the film's producer.

The success follows that of _Godzilla Minus One_, which won an Oscar for best visual effects and became the highest-grossing Japanese live-action film in the US and Canada despite a modest budget under $15 million.

_Godzilla Minus Zero_, made for less than $20 million, is expected to surpass its predecessor's record earnings of $57.1 million. The film has been well received by both critics and audiences, earning a 90% positive rating on Rotten Tomatoes.

Another international film, the Hindi-language epic _Ramayana: Rise of a Legend_, also had a strong opening weekend. The first part of the two-film story, based on Indian mythology, earned $8.7 million in North America and $32 million in India. It is the most expensive Indian film ever made.

Both _Godzilla_ and _Ramayana_ were made for IMAX, a cinema format that uses larger screens and advanced sound and picture technology. _Godzilla Minus Zero_ earned $21.3 million worldwide from IMAX screenings, accounting for 34% of its total box-office earnings.

The film's success could either boost excitement or set a high bar for the next Hollywood, English-language Godzilla movie, _Godzilla x Kong: Supernova,_ which is due in March.
