Have you ever watched a Korean drama on Netflix? If you have, you're certainly not alone!

According to Netflix, about 80% of its subscribers around the world have watched at least one Korean show or movie.

It's been 10 years since Netflix arrived in South Korea in 2016. And since then, Korean entertainment has become popular in many different parts of the world.

Since 2021, Netflix has shared weekly lists of its 10 most-watched shows and movies around the world, called the "Global Top 10." More than 210 different Korean shows and movies have appeared on these lists.

One of the biggest hits has been _Squid Game_. The series stayed on Netflix's Global Top 10 for 32 weeks, including nine weeks at number one.

But it's not just _Squid Game_ that's getting attention from people around the world.

In Japan, _Crash Landing on You_, a 2019 story about a South Korean woman who falls in love with a North Korean soldier, stayed in the country's Top 10 for 72 weeks.

In Bolivia, the 2009 show _Boys Over Flowers_ spent 49 weeks in the Top 10, while 2021's _Vincenzo_ stayed in Pakistan's Top 10 for 45 weeks.

One reason these shows have become so popular is that they're available in different languages. For example, the third season of _Squid Game_, released in 2025, was available with subtitles in 37 languages. People could also watch dubbed versions of the show in 24 languages.

So 10 years after Netflix arrived in South Korea, Korean entertainment is popular in countries all over the world.
